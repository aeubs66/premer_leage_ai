# ABSOLUTE RULE: NO DATA LEAKAGE

Data leakage prevention is one of the most important parts of this project.

When generating a feature for a historical game, pretend the system exists immediately before that game's kickoff.

The model must NEVER know anything from:

- the current game's final score
- future matches
- future player statistics
- future league position
- future Elo ratings
- future season totals
- final-season statistics
- statistics calculated using the current game's outcome

For rolling features:

use values such as:

shift(1)

before:

rolling(...)
expanding(...)
ewm(...)

when appropriate.

Write automated tests specifically designed to detect data leakage.

Include a document:

docs/data-leakage.md

that explains common leakage mistakes in football ML.

Do not hardcode example predictions and pretend they came from a trained model.

Do not call a model "accurate" without evaluation.

Do not optimize against the final test set.

Do not use future information.

Do not choose models based on test-set performance.

Do not confuse accuracy with probability quality.

Do not hide poor results.

This is a research/learning project, so negative results are valuable.
